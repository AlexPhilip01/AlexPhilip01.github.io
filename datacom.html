01 Overview

This simulation is built around how a managed service provider defends its clients — which is a meaningfully different problem from defending a single organisation. You are not protecting one network you know intimately; you are assessing an environment you have limited visibility into, then communicating risk to people who do not work for you.

That shaped the work. The investigation was only half of it; the other half was turning findings into a report a client could actually act on. In a real SOC, an analyst who can investigate but cannot write up the result clearly is only doing part of the job — the recommendation is the deliverable, not the packet capture.
