    How this campus was built

    The layout comes from a trace of the official campus map, corrected and anchored to the Ordnance Survey grid using the Historic England grid references of the two quad statues and the 1893 swimming pool. Founder's Building is reconstructed from its Grade I list entry. Façades of other buildings follow observations from a drive round the campus. Names are shown only where they could be verified; everything else is marked as estimated. Full notes are in rhul/README.md.
